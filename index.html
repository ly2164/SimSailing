V0.6 Wind, Crab & Live Notes Draft
Three fixes. The wind columns now bow gently and lean their arrowheads with the curve, so the stream reads as moving air. The rudder's sideways force now flips with the tack — swinging the helm to port shoves the hull to port, which is to leeward on one tack and to windward on the other, and the old fixed term pushed the boat downwind on both. The Tack readout was naming the leeward side and is now correct. The note card is a one-line teaser that opens into a wider panel on the chevron, and no longer auto-closes while you are reading it.

V0.5 Bow Leeway & Live Notes Draft
The leeway wedge was anchored to due north instead of the heading, so it sat in the wrong place whenever the bow was not pointing up — it now follows the bow. Notes became a slim card on the right that ignores touches and fades on its own, so they never block a drag. And the rudder now visibly bends the track: its side force scales with v² like a real rudder, moving leeway by 3–10° instead of the 0.0° it managed before.

Angle off the wind and leeway are both labelled at the bow.

Stops the sail whipping across the centreline the instant the bow passes due north while backing. V1.2 Backing the Sail Draft
Adds the transient you need to come through the wind: back the sail and the loaded rig pivots the hull even with no way on, so a tack that used to stall in the no-go zone now completes — 5.2 s from 50° off the wind, against a real 4–8 s.

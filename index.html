Sailing Physics Simulator — Build History

Each release adds a new version file; only the badge at the top of this page changes. Older versions are kept forever, so links that were shared never break. V0.1–V0.4 keep their original Chinese interface; V0.5 onward are English.
V0.6 Realistic Sailing Polar Current EN
Replaces the analytic speed curve with a measured dinghy polar. Upwind progress is now realistic: 45° off the wind gives 62% of top speed instead of 95%, top speed sits at beam reach (90°), and dead downwind is slower than a reach. Best velocity made good toward the wind lands at 47.5° making 43.9% of top speed, matching real dinghies. Also fixes a discontinuity where side force jumped from 0.05 to 1.0 at 90°.

SimSailing

The sailing simulator, version by version. Sail it, or open an older build to see how the physics and the handling changed.

V1.2 Backing the Sail Draft
Adds the transient you need to come through the wind: back the sail and the loaded rig pivots the hull even with no way on, so a tack that used to stall in the no-go zone now completes — 5.8 s from 45° off the wind, against a real 4–8 s. Not a release. The helm gesture is not settled and the turn is not yet predictable.

V1.1 Realistic Turn Rate Current
The boat answers the helm far too fast: a full rudder swung the bow through 90° in 1.2 s. Yaw authority now peaks near 6°/s, which is what a dinghy actually gives, and fades away as the boat slows.
